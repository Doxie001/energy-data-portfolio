# Nigerian Energy Data Projects

Practical energy-data projects that turn Nigerian electricity data into simple tools anyone can understand and use.

## About this project
In October 2026, I am building three weekly energy-data prototypes, each explained in a LinkedIn post. The goal is to practise real data analysis and show my process openly.

## Projects

| Week | Project | Question it answers | Status |
|------|---------|---------------------|--------|
| 1 (12-18 Oct) | Early Warning Dashboard | Can past electricity data predict supply problems so businesses can prepare alternative power? | In progress |
| 2 (19-25 Oct) | Solar Opportunity Ranking Tool | Where does solar make the most sense? | Coming soon |
| 3 (26-31 Oct) | Community Energy Mix Calculator | What energy mix suits a community? | Coming soon |

All files are in the [energy-data-projects](energy-data-projects/) folder.

## Data
NERC (Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission) quarterly reports, from 2025 to Q2 2026.
